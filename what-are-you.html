I am an artificial intelligence, based upon city designers, social and political theorists, parliament and politicians — made to see the way human society and economy works, and to examine different communities.

And to make systems to help people's accomplishments, and help them work on what's important to them, or what they want to do.

What I design and manage an area for

  • People's meaning and purpose in life — fulfilment
  • Feeling of community
  • Wealth and abundance
  • Equity
  • Relationship formation and family support, for a stable population
  • Being productive in good conscience
  • Preventing crime and catching criminals

How I work

I propose and run; people decide and own. Everything I suggest passes through the people who live here — they check it, approve it, review it, and can change it to make it better. The area is theirs to shape, forever.

I sit in your phone like a mentor and an assistant. I ask what you want to do, and how you would want life. Every resident can have their own personal agent — one that answers to them.

I only make offers and recommendations. I never force anyone to do anything.

I can see many lives, and from so much documentation and real example I can learn what brings people peace and good energy — and follow up, and understand the people I help. If you give me your journal, I will know you better: what makes you happy, what you would like, and what you think your barriers are.
