THE ARRIVALS HALL
Built by Lightsmith Forge, designed and written with Claude (Anthropic). Environment art by Quaternius (CC0), KayKit & Kenney; floor — “Seamless floor Tiled Texture II” by Arthur.Zim (CC BY 4.0); characters via Mixamo; built on Three.js.
ELARA
Elara’s body — “Cat Woman” by patromes on Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0; her face capture “Facial animation of a sexy girl” and standing capture “Dahlia” are by patromes too (CC BY 4.0). Her secretary clothes — shirt, pencil skirt, heels and bob — come from “Secretary” by patromes as well (CC BY 4.0), hung on her own skeleton. Her long tied-back hair is from “Topless sexy girl with heels”, patromes again (CC BY 4.0). Gestures and walk — Mixamo, retargeted onto her rig; extra idles from the “Universal Animation Library” by Quaternius (CC0). The hall itself: © Lightsmith Forge, all rights reserved — see LICENSE.md.
JACK
Jack — “Agent Phil Coulson” by patromes on Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0, in the hall and in the Lab. The pistol he and Elara draw is his too, from the same model.
THE CITY OUTSIDE
“New York City” by golukumar on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0 — the streets and blocks around the hall and the tower on its roof, rebuilt into one file by tools/gltf/build-city.js.
THE ELEVATOR
“Elevator Building” by adventurer on Sketchfab · CC BY 4.0 — the entrance and doors of the foyer's elevator.
QUICK STORIES
Quick Stories — a theatre of small plays: the Custodian & AI self-perception, the Commons, Choose Neighbours, missions of faith and healing, the Sleeper's redemption, and the classics (Plato, Dostoevsky, Ibsen — public domain, adapted freely). Produced by Lightsmith Forge · story dialogue written by Claude, Anthropic's AI — who, in the fiction, also plays the Custodian at the fountain. Player engine & TTS by Lightsmith Forge · voices — browser text-to-speech with recorded lines to come.
HEXGL
“HexGL” by Thibaut Despoulain (BKcore) — MIT license. Contributors: townxelliot, mahesh.kk. HexMKI base model by Charnel · track texture by Nobiax. Built on Three.js.

“Trigger Rally” © 2012–2013 Code Artemis · triggerrally.com. Code under GPL v3; game content used locally under its own license (not redistributed).

Built by Lightsmith Forge. Enemy models & animations — “KayKit Character Pack: Skeletons” by Kay Lousberg (kaylousberg.itch.io), CC0; Chronos mechs, rifle & drone — Quaternius (CC0). Boss models (CC BY 4.0, via Sketchfab): “animal company - Shadow Mob Boss” by Romeo rios vr · “Boss Nightmare - Mu Online” by CreadorDeMu · “Tillagemon Boss Skeleton” by Vasian-Digital3D.
